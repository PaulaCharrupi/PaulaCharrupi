# Hi, I'm Paula! 👋

### Chemical Engineering Undergraduate | Universidad de los Andes 

I'm interested in understanding how materials behave at the molecular level and how their structure and properties can be engineered for energy applications.

My main interests lie at the intersection of **electrochemistry, materials science, and scientific computing**, particularly in understanding charge transport and interfacial phenomena.

### Research Interests

- **Electrochemistry** — Electrochemical synthesis, conducting polymers, and charge-transfer processes.
- **Functional Materials** — Structure–property relationships, thin films, and material interfaces.
- **Energy Materials** — Perovskite solar cells, battery materials, and ionic/electronic transport.
- **Computational Research** — Scientific programming, numerical methods, and data-driven materials research.

### Current Research

I'm currently preparing an undergraduate research project focused on the **electrochemical synthesis of PEDOT thin films** for applications as hole-transport layers in inverted perovskite solar cells.

The project aims to investigate how electrodeposition conditions and film thickness influence the optical and electronic properties of PEDOT, with particular attention to hole extraction and transport.
